# Commercial Model

**Status:** Confirmed product direction  
**Date:** 2026-10-03

## B2B SaaS model

TrailerSaaS is intended to be sold to transportation/logistics companies on a recurring subscription basis.

The paying customer is the company/tenant, not the individual driver, yard spotter, dispatcher, or customer employee.

## Mobile application pricing

The iOS and Android applications are **free to download**.

There is no planned per-user purchase, in-app purchase, or mobile-app subscription for drivers or operational users.

Access to the mobile application is granted through an account belonging to an active TrailerSaaS tenant.

## Web application pricing

The Admin / Operations / Customer Portal is likewise accessed as part of the tenant's SaaS subscription.

Individual users do not purchase access separately.

## Billing principle

Commercial billing should happen at the company/tenant level through TrailerSaaS billing/contracting, outside the driver mobile workflow.

Potential SaaS pricing dimensions to evaluate later include:

- plan tier;
- number of managed trailers;
- number of drivers;
- usage/movements;
- hybrid base subscription + included limits.

No final pricing metric has been selected yet.

## Product implication

- App Store / Play Store listings should be free.
- Driver and Yard/Spotter apps must not require in-app purchase to unlock operational features.
- Mobile UI should not contain consumer-style upgrade/paywall flows.
- Tenant entitlement is checked server-side based on the subscribing organization.
- If self-service SaaS billing is added later, it should be handled at the tenant/account administration level rather than inside the driver app.
